Best practices

  • Group related actions with headers & dividers.
  • Reserve destructive actions to the bottom of the menu, in red.
  • Prefer data-bs-auto-close="outside" for forms and multi-select menus.

Accessibility

  • Bootstrap wires up role="menu", ARIA state and keyboard focus automatically.
  • Icon-only toggle buttons need an aria-label.
  • Keyboard: arrows to navigate, Enter to activate, Esc to close.
