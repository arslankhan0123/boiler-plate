How to use
  • Drag any header cell to reorder columns.
  • Grab the right edge of a header to resize.
  • Click the three-dot menu inside a header to pin left/right or hide the column.
  • Change Group by to fold rows into collapsible categories.
  • The footer aggregation follows the toolbar selector.
Export & bulk
  • CSV and JSON export ship out-of-the-box.
  • PDF and Excel are stub buttons — plug in jsPDF or SheetJS in production.
  • Bulk update sets one field to the same value across every visible row.
