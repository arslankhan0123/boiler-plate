Best practices

  • Use consistent padding and border-radius across the app.
  • Group similar cards in a grid — avoid mixing heights within a row.
  • Anchor at most one primary action per card.

Accessibility

  • Wrap interactive cards in a proper button/link, not a div with an onclick.
  • Give each card a meaningful heading for screen readers.
  • Ensure focus outlines are visible when the card is interactive.
